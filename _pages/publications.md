## Atlas-DeePC: Learning Behavioral Atlases for Predictive Control

Dian Jin, **Zhiyuan Jin**, and Jeremy Coulson

_Under review at L4DC 2027._ · [Project page](https://dianjin-frederick.github.io/Atlas-DeePC/) · [Paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18266)

Atlas-DeePC replaces a single global data matrix with an atlas of small local subspaces learned offline from recorded data. Online, it selects the chart matching recent measurements and solves a DeePC problem whose size depends on the chart dimension rather than the dataset size.

## Characterizing Identifiability and Generalization for Inverse Receding-Horizon Linear-Quadratic Regulator Problems

**Zhiyuan Jin**, Jingqi Li, and David Fridovich-Keil

_Under review at ACC 2027._ · [Paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14053)

This work studies when first-action observations identify the objective of a receding-horizon controller, and when an inferred objective generalizes to unseen states. We characterize the information available in observed behavior and the role of the geometry of observed states in predicting actions.
